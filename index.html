Who we are

Prayer & Fire was founded on September 13, 2024 as a global Christian movement devoted to prayer, faith, unity, missions and service across nations.

What we are

A global Christian movement impacting nations and new generations through prayer, unity, faith and collaboration across cultures and countries.

What we do

  • Prayer gatherings and intercession
  • Global ministry connection
  • Missions and community outreach
  • Educational and food support initiatives
  • Faith-based resources and service
